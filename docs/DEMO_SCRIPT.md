# Demo script

Three minutes. Record on a phone beside a laptop with Basescan open.

**0:00** Hook. Contractors wait months to get paid. Clients pay for work that is not done.

**0:15** Client uploads the sample stage breakdown. The Mini App shows the ten Lekki stages, the ₦45,000,000 sum, and 30,000.00 USDC at the locked ₦1,500 rate. The contractor accepts the same summary.

**0:45** Part-payment through the first-floor slab. Confirm the stages covered and the exact USDC. Open the Basescan transaction once a deployer key has broadcast one. Until then, say the escrow tests prove the quote and the chain deploy is the next cut.

**1:05** Subcontractor joins with an 8-character code. The bot says the payment account is ready and hides the address under Advanced. `/switch` lists only that user's projects.

**1:25** Live camera on the pour. A gallery photo is rejected. The assessor holds the slab at 0.78 for the consultant. The bot says the agent paid $0.01 for the weather check. The paid route returns 402 until that header is present.

**1:55** A stage over the ₦5,000,000 cap. The client taps Approve. USDC is released once. The letterhead certificate is the PDF from `packages/pdf`.

**2:25** The resolved foundation dispute, and the open soakaway variation with its response clock.

**2:45** Point at this repo: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, the Foundry tests, and the seed.
