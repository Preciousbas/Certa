# Demo script

Three minutes. Record on a phone beside a laptop with Etherscan open.

The local rehearsal lives in `scripts/e2e-demo/` (gitignored). Run `pnpm demo:e2e` to walk the database path without inventing chain hashes.

**0:00** Hook. Contractors wait months to get paid. Clients pay for work that is not done.

**0:15** Client uploads the sample stage breakdown. The Mini App shows the ten Lekki stages, the sample contract sum, and the USDC amount at the locked rate. The contractor accepts the same summary.

**0:45** Part-payment through the first-floor slab. Confirm the stages covered and the exact USDC. Open the Etherscan transaction once a deployer key has broadcast one. Until then, say the escrow tests prove the quote and the chain deploy is the next cut.

**1:05** Subcontractor joins with an 8-character code. The bot says the payment account is ready and hides the address under Advanced. `/switch` lists only that user's projects.

**1:25** Live camera on the pour. A gallery photo is rejected. The assessor holds the slab for the consultant when the score is below 0.85 or the stage is over the auto cap. Money stays locked. The weather route answers 402 until a real payment signature settles. The daily note names a receipt only when `x402_calls` stores a 32-byte hash.

**1:55** A stage over the ₦5,000,000 cap. The client taps Approve. USDC is released once a real release tx exists. The letterhead certificate is the PDF from `packages/pdf`, and only after that hash is stored.

**2:25** The resolved foundation dispute, and the open soakaway variation with its response clock.

**2:45** Point at this repo: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, the Foundry tests, and the seed.

## Manual checklist (under five minutes once keys are set)

1. Client locks a part-payment through stage 2. Confirm screen shows Waiting on chain until a real tx hash exists.
2. Sub joins with an 8-character code. Payment account ready. Address under Advanced.
3. Live camera on the pour. Gallery photo rejected.
4. Verdict stored. Below threshold or over the auto cap goes to the consultant. Money stays locked.
5. Consultant approves. Approving does not pay. A pour review tries the weather payment when the payer key and pay-to address are set.
6. Release only after a real escrow release tx. Certificate only with that hash.
7. Telegram notices only when the bot token is set.
8. Open Etherscan for fund, release, and weather receipt.
